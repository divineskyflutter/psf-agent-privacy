Privacy Policy

PSF Agent mobile app — Parivar Suraksha Foundation

Effective date: 24 November 2026  ·  Last updated: 24 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information the PSF Agent mobile app (package com.psffoundation.agent, the “App”) collects, why we collect it, how we protect it, and the choices you have. The App is published by Parivar Suraksha Foundation (“PSF”, “we”, “us”, “our”), a not-for-profit company based in Surat, Gujarat, India, and is used by PSF agents to register members and submit their membership applications.

By using the App you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information we collect

We collect only the information needed to let agents register members and to keep membership records accurate. Most of it is information that the agent enters in the App about themselves or about the member being registered.

a) Agent details

  • The mobile number and password used to sign in to the App
  • The agent’s profile shown on the Home screen (such as name, photo, mobile number and age) and the list and count of members registered by that agent

b) Member registration and profile details

  • Name (first, middle/father’s name, surname) — including the Hindi and Gujarati versions of the name and address, which the App can generate (the text typed is sent to our server to produce them)
  • Gender, date of birth, marital status
  • Mobile number(s)
  • Address (address line, village, taluka, district, state) and occupation

c) Identity documents, photos and signature

  • Member’s profile photo
  • Aadhaar number and photos of the front and back of the Aadhaar card
  • PAN number and a photo of the PAN card
  • The member’s signature — drawn on screen, or an image chosen from the gallery

d) Nominee details

  • For each nominee: name (including Hindi/Gujarati), date of birth, relationship, share, photo, Aadhaar number and photos, and a passbook/cheque image

The agent is responsible for making sure the member and nominee have permitted their details to be given to us.

e) Health declaration

  • Answers given in the membership health declaration (for example whether the member has a serious illness, surgery, regular medication or allergies, habits such as tobacco or alcohol, and any other details added). This is sensitive information and is used only to process the membership application.

f) Application records

  • Registration progress, declarations and rules accepted, and the final submitted application, which can be viewed or saved as a PDF

g) Information from your device

  • Photos you choose or take. The App receives the pictures the agent picks from the gallery or captures with the camera for a document or profile photo.
  • Data stored on your phone. Your chosen language and theme, and your sign-in session details (tokens and agent/member IDs), are kept on your phone. Sign-in tokens are kept in the phone’s encrypted secure storage and removed when you log out. Images shown in the App may be temporarily cached on your phone.
  • Technical data. Like any app that talks to a server, our servers receive your IP address and basic request information when the App connects.
  • Connection checks. To tell you when you are offline, the App makes small requests to public connectivity-test servers. These servers can see your IP address; no personal information is sent.

What we do not collect

The App does not use or collect your location, contacts, SMS messages, call logs, microphone, calendar, or advertising ID. It contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

3. How we use your information

  • To authenticate agents and show them the members they have registered
  • To register members, verify identity and process membership applications and nominee details
  • To contact agents or members about an application or corrections needed
  • To provide support, keep our records accurate, and prevent fraud or misuse
  • To meet legal, accounting and regulatory obligations

We do not use your information for advertising and we do not sell it.

5. Sharing your information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only:

  • with the technology and hosting providers that run the App’s servers for us, under obligations to keep it confidential and secure;
  • with government, regulatory or law-enforcement authorities when the law requires it; and
  • with anyone else only if you have given your consent.

6. How we protect your information

  • Data between the App and our servers is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Sign-in tokens are kept in the phone’s secure storage, and the App’s data is excluded from Android cloud backup.
  • Access to member records is limited to authorised persons of the Foundation and its service providers.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your password private and log out on shared devices.

7. How long we keep your information

We keep information for as long as the agent account or membership is active and for as long afterwards as is needed for the purposes above or required by law (for example, accounting and regulatory records). When a deletion request is approved (see section 9), we delete the personal information concerned, except what we must keep by law.

8. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law (including India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you can:

  • ask to see the details we hold about you or about a member you registered;
  • ask us to correct or update inaccurate details;
  • withdraw your consent by requesting deletion of your data (see section 9);
  • raise a complaint or grievance with us at the address below.

To use these rights, email psk4mail@gmail.com from the email/phone details you are registered with. We aim to respond within 30 days.

9. Deleting your account and data

To request deletion of an agent account, or of a member’s data submitted through the App:

  1. Email psk4mail@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data”.
  2. Include the registered mobile number (and the member’s name, if the request is about a member).
  3. We will verify the request and complete the deletion within 30 days, then confirm to you.

What is deleted

The profile, identity details, photos and signature, nominee details, health declaration and application records linked to the account or member.

What we may have to keep

Some records (for example financial, accounting or regulatory records, or information needed to resolve a dispute or prevent fraud) may be kept for the period the law requires. They are used only for that purpose and are deleted when the period ends.

Deletion cannot be undone.

10. Children

The App is meant for adults (18 years or older). Information about a minor, such as a nominee, is provided by an adult who confirms they are entitled to give it. We do not knowingly allow children to use the App. If you believe a child’s data was submitted wrongly, please contact us and we will remove it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when new features are added to the App. The latest version will always be available at this address and its “last updated” date will change. If a change is significant we will also tell you in the App.
